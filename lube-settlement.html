2. Scheme rules applied

Scheme dates: 01–31 October 2026 (three periods)Minimum customer discount: ₹20 / ₹30 / ₹31 per litre by periodJCB / Dealer share: 50% / 50% of scheme support

Quantity determines the highest qualifying slab within the invoice date period. Minimum actual discount is ₹20/L (1–12 Oct), ₹30/L (13–21 Oct), and ₹31/L (22–31 Oct). For 22–31 Oct, Slab-1 requires Hydraulic Oil only; Slabs 2–4 require at least two different lube products. Eligible litres are capped at 200. JCB pays 50% of scheme support; dealer bears the balance of actual customer discount.
